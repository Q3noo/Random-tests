## Q1. How do 2D souls-likes replace what lock-on does in 3D (facing, strafing, turning around)?

### Takeaway
In side view there is no lock-on, strafing, or circling: facing is simply left/right, and the "get around the enemy" function of 3D circle-strafing is replaced by **(a) a jump button** and **(b) rolling through/past enemies**. Reviewers repeatedly name the loss of circling as the main adjustment, and judging whether a roll will carry you *past* an enemy is a known 2D pain point.

### Cited Findings
- Salt and Sanctuary: "The move to 2D means there's no sidestepping, or circling the enemy using a lock-on. In order to compensate, there's a dedicated jump button which comes in handy for both combat and exploration." (High-Med) — [ZTGD review](https://ztgd.com/salt-and-sanctuary-ps4-review/)
- Kotaku on Salt and Sanctuary: losing the ability to circle enemies "takes some getting used to," but the game offers new options instead; the jump lets you get around enemies and dodge attacks, complementing the roll; it is hard in 2D to judge whether a roll will carry you past an enemy, and it often ends with you running into them. (Med) — [Kotaku review](https://kotaku.com/1765579567)
- Ska Studios' framing: Salt and Sanctuary "began with a pretty simple idea: how would Dark Souls play if it was reimagined in our 2D hand-drawn action style?"; a named core challenge was merging build diversity with action-centric gameplay. (High, primary) — [PlayStation Blog launch post](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2016/03/15/seek-out-salt-sanctuary-on-ps4-from-today) (attribution to this specific PS Blog post per search summary)
- Michelle Silva (Ska co-founder): the team thought of Dark Souls as a 3D version of 2D Castlevania, especially Symphony of the Night; combat movement echoes that game. (High, primary quote relayed) — [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/94159/salt-and-sanctuary-the-2d-soulslike-inspired-by-castlevania)
- 3D reference point for "dodge changes with lock-on": Lies of P gives a dodge **roll** when not locked on and a shorter dodge **hop** when locked on; the roll goes farther and has a more recognizable animation, so it's not just cosmetic. (Med) — [J. Margaris, "Dodging in the Lies of P demo"](https://jmargaris.substack.com/p/dodging-in-the-lies-of-p-demo-kinda)
- A pre-Elden-Ring observation from a souls-like developer: "At the time – this was before Elden Ring had come out – they hadn't introduced any sort of jumping mechanics [in the Souls games]" (Kristala dev Brutman; Kristala began as a 2D side-scroller). (Med) — [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/tired-of-waiting-for-bloodborne-2-these-devs-decided-to-make-their-own-now-its-sekiro-with-cat-ninjas-and-assassins-creed-dna)

### Inferences
- In 2D, "lock-on" collapses into a single rule: **which way am I facing after I cross the boss?** The two standard options are (a) facing only changes on stick input (classic Castlevania/Salt) or (b) auto-face the target when idle/at roll end (lock-on-like). For a prototype that lacks lock-on and has a boss that tracks, auto-turn at roll end (or a very fast turn on input) approximates ER's locked-on roll; this is an inference, not sourced.
- Because Lies of P makes the *locked-on* dodge shorter (a hop), a 2D analogue is: roll = long, crosses enemies; backstep = short, never crosses. That maps cleanly onto the missing backstep in the prototype.
- Jump is the closest 2D analogue to "sidestepping a vertical/horizontal threat," and Elden Ring itself legitimized jumping as evasion (per the Kristala quote, earlier Souls had none).

## Q2. Dodge design in 2D: roll through vs. away, distance/i-frames, backstep, jump-as-evasion, sweeps vs. overheads

### Takeaway
The strongest design critique (Problem Machine, "Rolled Flat") is that a 3D-style roll in 2D degrades into **pure i-frame spam** that makes walking obsolete, because 2D hitboxes are crude and the roll's 3D purpose (shrinking/relocating your body around an attack) disappears. Successful 2D souls-likes either (1) give the roll generous i-frames and accept roll-through as the main answer (Ender Lilies, Blasphemous slide), (2) shift primary defense to parry/deflect (Nine Sols, Grime, Blasphemous), or (3) add jump as a second evasion axis with a stamina tax (Salt and Sanctuary, Death's Gambit). Sweeps-vs-overheads is handled by **jump-over (low/sweeping) vs. roll-through (tall/overhead)**, and in Nine Sols by a special aerial parry for red attacks.

### Cited Findings
- Problem Machine, "Rolled Flat" (Patreon essay, 17 Aug 2022): in Dark Souls rolling moves you quickly and changes your body profile, but "in 2D, hit-boxes tend to be crude and all-encompassing so just shrinking one's hit-box for a moment is of limited utility"; the roll's utility "becomes entirely about the invincibility," which in Dark Souls was a side benefit; "balancing dodge-rolling against standard movement is very tricky, and often moving around without rolling becomes nearly obsolete"; the attack/dodge loop becomes repetitive and undercuts the positioning that makes 2D action appealing; players need clear information about how far the roll goes, when they regain control, and when a roll can/can't be started. (Med; snippet-only) — [Problem Machine, "Rolled Flat"](https://www.patreon.com/ProblemMachine/posts/rolled-flat-70625048)
- A devblog for an indie action game lets the character pass **through enemies** during the roll but **not** through normally blocking geometry. (Low-Med) — [ModDB devblog "The Art of Dodge"](https://www.moddb.com/news/devblog-the-art-of-dodge)
- Blasphemous (1): defensive tools are a **dodge slide** (with i-frames) and a **parry**; stronger enemy strikes can still knock the Penitent One back through a parry; rosary beads can lengthen the dodge or widen the parry window (i.e., dodge length and parry window are tunable stats). (Med) — [PC Invasion](https://pcinvasion.com/?p=197672)
- Blasphemous II: Destructoid found that even boss fights "which looked impossible without a dedicated block button" could be handled by dodging with the heavy Veredicto; a boss guide recommends using the slide to dodge the Faceless One's attacks. (Med) — [Destructoid review](https://www.destructoid.com/review-blasphemous-2/); [boss guide](https://onemoregame.ph/?p=117134)
- Ender Lilies: the dodge "has a generous number of invincibility frames," useful when surrounded; a guide says "Much like Dark Souls…dodging is genuinely overpowered in Ender Lilies." (Med; outlet attribution uncertain between guide pages) — [Prima Games beginner's guide](https://primagames.com/gaming/ender-lilies-beginners-guide-combat-bosses/2); [PC Invasion version](https://pcinvasion.com/ender-lilies-beginners-guide-combat-bosses/2)
- Death's Gambit: "A single roll used up about one-fourth of my stamina, and swinging heavy weapons didn't do it any favors"; stamina "can feel too restrictive in some fights as you're torn between jumping or dodging" (i.e., jump and roll draw from the same stamina pool). (Med; outlet attribution uncertain among reviews) — [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/deaths-gambit-review); [GameSpot review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/deaths-gambit-review-a-maddening-mission/1900-6416965/)
- Mandragora (2025, 2D): stamina drain is severe enough that draining it "slows down your movement and you will be unable to dodge, as utilising the evasive manoeuvres also has a hit on the stamina bar." (Med; outlet attribution uncertain) — [RPG Site review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/17387-mandragora-whispers-of-the-witch-tree-review); [Inverse review](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/mandragora-whispers-of-the-witch-tree-review)
- Hollow Knight: the base dash has **no** i-frames; i-frames only come with the late-game Shade Cloak, whose i-frames cover the full ~0.4 s dash; base dash cooldown ~0.6 s (~0.4 s with Dashmaster). Shade Cloak cooldown is **1.5 s** per the community wiki vs **2 s** per Fextralife (conflict; wiki considered more reliable); the wiki says environmental hazards still damage you during it. (Med-High) — [Hollow Knight Wiki: Shade Cloak](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Shade_Cloak); [Fextralife](https://hollowknight.wiki.fextralife.com/Shade+Cloak); [RogueRanker guide](https://rogueranker.com/shade-cloak-hollow-knight/)
- Nine Sols: a **jumping parry** counters certain red (unblockable) attacks, lifted from Sekiro's aerial parry ("exactly how the same aerial parry worked in Sekiro"). (Med) — [GamesRadar review](https://gamesradar.com/games/action/nine-sols-review)
- Nine Sols: you can only parry one attack at a time, so dodging is "your best bet if you're surrounded"; guides suggest dodging away when two enemies attack simultaneously. (Med) — [Ginx parry explainer](https://www.ginx.tv/en/?p=66621); [GameSkinny tips](https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/beginner-tips-for-nine-sols/)

### Inferences
- **Roll through vs. away:** 2D souls-likes overwhelmingly let rolls pass through enemies (otherwise the boss body walls you). The Kotaku complaint (can't tell if roll carries you past) implies the fix is a **clear, fixed roll distance longer than the boss's body width + margin**, plus collision disabled only during the roll (ModDB pattern).
- **Backstep:** Problem Machine's "walking becomes obsolete" risk argues for giving the prototype a short, cheap backstep (few i-frames, never crosses the boss) as the "spacing" tool and keeping the roll as the "commit/cross" tool, mirroring Lies of P's roll-vs-hop split.
- **Sweeps vs. overheads on one axis:** the cleanest 2D grammar is: low horizontal sweep = jump over (or jump-parry, Nine Sols-style); tall overhead/slam = roll through or roll away; thrust = parry/block. This requires adding jump; without it, all boss attacks must be roll-answerable, which is exactly the i-frame-spam degeneracy Problem Machine describes.
- **Stamina sharing between jump and roll** (Death's Gambit) creates meaningful choice but critics found it frustrating; a lower jump cost than roll cost is a reasonable compromise (inference).
- Hollow Knight shows a 2D action game can skip i-frame dodges entirely and rely on positioning + jump; this is the opposite pole from Souls and generally reads as "platformer combat," not "souls combat" (inference).

### Gaps
- No sourced i-frame frame counts or roll distances for Salt and Sanctuary, Blasphemous, Ender Lilies, Death's Gambit, Nine Sols, or Grime (wikis that might have them were not reachable).
- No source found on dedicated **backstep** in any 2D souls-like (I could not confirm whether Salt and Sanctuary has one).
- Unverified (background knowledge, not confirmed this session): Dark Souls 3 / Elden Ring standard roll is commonly cited as ~13 i-frames at 30 fps (~0.43 s); the report writer should take ER numbers from the ER-focused research notes, not from here.

## Q3. Which Elden Ring-defining mechanics have been adapted in 2D, and how?

### Takeaway
Parry-into-riposte/stance damage is the most commonly adapted mechanic (Blasphemous, Grime, Nine Sols, Ender Lilies, Mandragora), usually **Sekiro-flavored** rather than Elden Ring's guard counter. Charged heavies exist (Blasphemous' Mea Culpa charge, inherited by Blasphemous II's Veredicto). Jumping is native to 2D (and pre-dates ER's jump). I found no sourced 2D adaptations of ER-specific jump attacks (as a stance-damage tool), guard counters (block-then-heavy), walking while healing, delayed/feint boss swings, or boss turn-tracking.

### Cited Findings
- **Parry/riposte replaced by absorb (Grime):** the parry button absorbs enemy essence; the timing-based move "replaces the familiar parry and riposte in Dark Souls"; success yields healing "breath" energy; many bosses can only be defeated through judicious use of it; against bosses you can absorb projectiles and even limbs, dealing big damage and temporarily removing an appendage. Stamina is renamed "breath." (High; multiple reviews) — [NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/grime-review-hideous-tortured-and-impossibly-moreish-3008491); [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/125923/grime-review-chiseled-polished-metroidvania); [GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/destroy-absorb-grow-grime-review)
- **Stance/posture analogue (Nine Sols):** a slightly mistimed parry still deflects but leaves grey/red "internal damage" that slowly regenerates unless you're hit again (then it becomes permanent); internal damage alone cannot kill you; every blocked attack builds Qi, spent to attach talismans that explode, dealing large damage and **locking in** the enemy's internal damage. Enemies also take internal damage that regenerates if you stop attacking. A Steam user argues it is not the same as Sekiro's posture system. (Med-High) — [GameSkinny review](https://www.gameskinny.com/reviews/nine-sols-review-a-tale-of-sci-fi-taoism-and-sekiro-parries/); [GameSkinny parry guide](https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/how-to-parry-in-nine-sols/); [Ginx](https://www.ginx.tv/en/?p=66621)
- Red Candle's own pitch: "hand-drawn 2D action platformer with Sekiro-inspired deflection-focused combat." (High, primary) — [PC Gamer announcement](https://www.pcgamer.com/devotion-studio-red-candle-is-working-on-a-taopunk-action-platformer-with-sekiro-inspired-combat)
- Nine Sols parry cue: press timed to the enemy's **white glow flash**. (Med) — [Ginx](https://www.ginx.tv/en/?p=66621)
- **Poise break via parry (Ender Lilies):** parry comes from a relic (Fretia's Ring) and works by pressing RT **while stationary**; a perfect parry nullifies the full hit and visibly empties the enemy's poise meter, though enemies can recover "almost immediately." (Med) — [Prima Games guide](https://primagames.com/gaming/ender-lilies-beginners-guide-combat-bosses/2)
- **Charged heavy (Blasphemous II):** Veredicto, the heavy censer, is so heavy "you can't even parry with it" and "inherits Mea Culpa's charge attack"; it can be set alight with Fervour. Ruego al Alba (sword) is the balanced weapon that can block and counter "provided your timing is on point." (Med) — [Superjump Magazine](https://superjumpmagazine.com/blasphemous-2-the-best-metroidvania-of-2023); [GameSpot review](https://Gamespot.com/reviews/blasphemous-2-review-unholy-pilgrimage/1900-6418101/); [Prima Games review](https://primagames.com/featured/blasphemous-2-review-hallowed-be-thy-game)
- Weapon-weight tradeoff in Blasphemous II: Veredicto is "too slow to handle certain enemies" yet PCGamesN felt "the sheer effectiveness of swinging a flaming censer" undercut weapon variety (i.e., the heavy option was overtuned). (Med) — [PCGamesN review](https://www.pcgamesn.com/blasphemous-2/review)
- Weapon archetypes (Death's Gambit): greatswords slow but devastating, scythes hit a huge area around you, daggers fast and combo-heavy; attacks "need a short wind up before the strike, placing an emphasis on timing over button-mashing." (Med; outlet attribution uncertain) — [PC Gamer review](https://pcgamer.com/deaths-gambit-review)
- **Perfect parry** highlighted as a distinctive feature of Mandragora (2025), which a preview framed as "speaking the same design language as Dark Souls, Elden Ring, and more." (Med) — [Epic Games Store hands-on](https://store.epicgames.com/news/mandragora-whispers-of-the-witch-tree-hands-on/)
- 2D games that explicitly invoke Elden Ring: Mandragora (above); Darkblade Ascent's devs: "Elden Ring and Soulslike games in general, with a touch of Dark Messiah of Might & Magic—those were our initial inspirations" (search results did not confirm its camera perspective). (Med) — [Epic Games Store: Darkblade Ascent](https://store.epicgames.com/news/darkblade-ascent-brings-the-roguelike-to-the-soulslike)
- Critics explicitly route Elden Ring players to Nine Sols: PC Gamer headline "Finished Shadow of the Erdtree and looking for more punishment? Get Nine Sols…" (High) — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/finished-shadow-of-the-erdtree-and-looking-for-more-punishment-get-nine-sols-the-soulslike-metroidvania-so-good-it-made-me-stop-pining-for-hollow-knight-silksong)

### Inferences
- **Guard counter in 2D:** no 2D game found copies ER's exact "block, then heavy button" guard counter; Ruego al Alba's block-and-counter is the nearest precedent. For the prototype, a guard counter is cheap to add (block state + a heavy press within N frames after a blocked hit → fast stance-damaging swing) and is more ER-specific than a Sekiro parry.
- **Stance break + critical:** Ender Lilies (poise meter emptied by perfect parry) and Nine Sols (talisman locks internal damage) both make the stance meter **visible**; the prototype already has poise break, so the missing ER piece is the **critical/riposte prompt** when the boss is staggered (a big, uninterruptible, high-hitstop punish).
- **Charged heavy with hyperarmor:** Blasphemous' charge attack and MH's charged greatsword (Q5) both pair the charge with a strong impact payoff; overtuning the heavy (PCGamesN on Veredicto) is a known failure mode, so stamina cost/recovery should keep it a commitment.
- **Jump attacks:** in 2D, jump already exists for traversal; ER's innovation is that a jump attack deals bonus poise/stance damage and lets you hop over sweeps. A 2D jump-heavy (downward slam) that does extra poise damage would be the direct port (inference; no 2D source found).

### Gaps
- No sources found for 2D adaptations of: walking while healing (ER) vs. rooted healing; sprinting; delayed/feint boss attacks; boss input-reading or turn-tracking; ER-style jump-attack stance bonus.
- Salt and Sacrifice's specific mechanical changes were not retrieved (only framing as "part Dark Souls, part Monster Hunter": [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/salt-and-sacrifice-is-part-dark-souls-part-monster-hunter)).
- Ender Magnolia (2025) combat changes not found.

## Q4. Game-by-game notes (Salt, Blasphemous, Hollow Knight/Silksong, Ender Lilies, Death's Gambit, Elderand, Nine Sols, Grime, Mandragora)

### Takeaway
The 2D souls-likes that critics call most "Souls-feeling" share: **stamina-gated committed actions, telegraphed boss attacks answered by i-frame roll and/or timed parry, and heavy weapons with wind-up.** The ones called weightless or stiff are faulted for lack of hitstop, detached attacks, or stiff movement.

### Cited Findings
- **Salt and Sanctuary (2016, Ska Studios):** roll dodges and "liberal use of shields" carry over from Dark Souls (GameSpot); roll and parry as defense, light and heavy attacks as offense (Softpedia); shield parry (Gamereactor). - **Salt and Sacrifice (2022):** framed as part Dark Souls, part Monster Hunter (mage hunting). (Med) — [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/salt-and-sacrifice-is-part-dark-souls-part-monster-hunter); [NME](https://www.nme.com/features/salt-and-sacrifice-offers-an-intriguing-blend-of-dark-souls-and-monster-hunter-3072863)
- **Blasphemous (2019):** dodge slide with i-frames + parry; heavy enemy strikes can push through parry; rosary beads extend dodge / widen parry window. ### Inferences
- Two lineages exist: **Dark Souls lineage** (roll + stamina + shields + heavy weapons: Salt, Death's Gambit, Mandragora, Elderand) and **Sekiro lineage** (parry/deflect-first, posture-like meters: Nine Sols, Grime, partly Blasphemous II's sword). Elden Ring sits closer to the DS lineage but adds jump, guard counter, and stance-break crits; a prototype aiming at *ER* feel should keep roll + stamina as primary and add parry/guard counter as secondary, not primary.

## Q5. Game-feel references for weighty melee: hitstop, input buffer, animation canceling, screen shake, sound

### Takeaway
Hitstop should scale with impact (rule-of-thumb ~2–4 f light, ~5–8 f medium, longer for heavy; >~20 f reads as a bug), freeze attacker and target together, and is noticeably missed when removed (Monster Hunter Wilds beta). Input buffers in fighting games run ~7–9 frames; Elden Ring's long "remember everything" buffer is a frequent complaint (queued attacks firing after a dodge). Screen shake is best driven by a decaying "trauma" value with shake ∝ trauma² (Eiserloh, GDC 2016). Sound "weight" comes from a whoosh lead-in plus a layered transient/body/tail impact aligned to the contact frame.

### Cited Findings
**Hitstop / hitlag**
- Sakurai (Famitsu column): **hitstop** (freeze on impact, mainly a visual effect emphasizing impact) is distinct from **hitstun** (how long the victim is locked out). (High) — [Source Gaming translation](https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/)
- Smash Bros. hitlag for a 15%-damage move with no modifiers: 10 f (64), 8 f (Melee), 10 f (Brawl/Smash 4), 15 f (Ultimate); Melee hitlag capped at 20 f; Brawl introduced per-move hitlag multipliers. (High, community wiki with datamined formulas) — [SmashWiki: Hitlag](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Hitlag)
- Rule-of-thumb: light hits ~2–4 frames, medium ~5–8, scale with weight; beyond ~20 frames "starts to feel like a bug." (Low; tutorial site) — [GameJuice: hit-stop frame trick](https://gamejuice.pages.dev/articles/hit-stop-frame-trick)
- Street Fighter V reportedly uses **8 / 12 / 15** frames hitstop for light / medium / heavy; Devil May Cry 5 applies hit pause only to heavy attacks. (Low; relayed from a developer's Twitter thread, could not be fetched/verified) — [Jason de Heras thread](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1385104062157254660)
- Freezing **both** attacker and target keeps the hit feeling mutual; freezing only the target looks like a hiccup. (Low) — [FreeGameSprites](https://freegamesprites.com/es/news/game-feel-2d-combat-juice-tricks)
- Monster Hunter: hitstop is the small lag felt when a charged attack (Level 2+) or combo connects; in the **Monster Hunter Wilds open beta**, hitstop was missing from Great Sword charged attacks and the True Charged Slash "felt much less impactful than in earlier titles." (Med) — [Deltia's Gaming](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=113987)
- Counterpoint: some players request an option to **disable** hit stop on melee/parry animations (Space Marine 2 community idea board). (Low; signals hitstop on *defensive* actions can feel like lag) — [Focus Entertainment community](https://community.focus-entmt.com/focus-entertainment/space-marine-2/ideas/7565-add-in-game-setting-to-disable-hit-stop-effect)

**Input buffering / animation canceling**
- Elden Ring "buffers your inputs, remembering what you pressed during an animation and executing it the moment the animation ends"; a popular mod ("No Input Queue") discards those inputs instead. (Med) — [Nexus Mods: No Input Queue](https://www.nexusmods.com/eldenring/mods/11143)
- ResetEra thread "The input buffer in Elden Ring is the worst I've ever experienced": posters say the series has "no animation cancelling system"; a dodge replaces a queued attack "but then after I come out of the dodge, the attack happens anyway"; the buffer seems to open at the start of a stagger animation, and "tightening up the buffer window would solve this"; queued items "in the back of the queue can be overwritten." (Low-Med; anecdotal, some posts conflict) — [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/the-input-buffer-in-elden-ring-is-the-worst-ive-ever-experienced.564406/)

**Screen shake**
- Vlambeer's "The Art of Screenshake" (Jan Willem Nijman, INDIGO Classes / Control Conference Amsterdam) takes a dull side-scrolling shooter and adds ~30 small tweaks. (High) — [Game Developer summary](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/vlambeer-co-founder-shares-advice-on-building-better-action-games). A fan recreation's step list includes: enemy hit reaction + knockback, camera lerp, screenshake, **player knockback**, **hit pause**, weapon recoil, permanence (smoke/corpses). (Med; order may not match talk) — [DK Liao recreation devlog](https://dkliao.itch.io/the-art-of-screenshake-recreation/devlog/451576/quick-breakdown-of-all-the-effects); [Univ. Bayreuth slides](https://medienwissenschaft.uni-bayreuth.de/wp-content/uploads/GxD-10-Game-Feel.pdf)
- Squirrel Eiserloh, GDC 2016 "Math for Game Programmers: Juicing Your Cameras With Math" covers camera shake and smoothing (High) — [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1023146/Math-for-Game-Programmers-Juicing). Common implementations: a **trauma** value 0–1 that decays over time; shake amplitude = trauma² (or ³) sampled through Perlin noise (e.g., 0.2² = 0.04 vs 0.9² = 0.81, so small hits barely shake while big hits shake hard). Squaring is a common implementation choice; not confirmed from the talk text itself. (Med) — [Bevy 2D screen shake example](https://bevy.org/examples/camera/2d-screen-shake/); [KidsCanCode Godot recipe](https://kidscancode.org/godot_recipes/3.x/2d/screen_shake/); [GameJuice screen-shake guide](https://gamejuice.pages.dev/articles/screen-shake-guide)

**Sound and weight**
- Impact layering: sharp transient (highs) + heavier body (mids/low-mids, "weight and power") + short tail, with sub-bass rumble; pair a "thwack" with a "whoosh"; a punch without a preceding whoosh "feels static and lifeless"; start the swoosh slightly *before* visible movement; impacts aligned to contact, typically 1–2 frames before the visual peak; vary samples rather than repeating one at one volume; powerful attacks get longer tails, rapid combos lighter, clearer sounds; keep dry weapon sound separate from environmental reverb tail. (Med; audio-blog guidance, outlet attribution uncertain between the two) — [Pixflow](https://pixflow.net/blog/punch-impact-sound-effects-for-fight-scenes/); [Violet Recording](https://violetrecording.com/how-to-design-weapon-sounds/)

### Inferences
- **Concrete starting values for the prototype** (inferred from the ranges above, at 60 fps): light hit 3–4 f (~50–67 ms); light combo finisher 5–6 f; uncharged heavy 7–8 f (~120–133 ms); fully charged heavy 10–14 f (~170–230 ms); boss heavy hitting player 6–10 f; poise-break/critical 14–18 f plus a slow-mo or camera punch; cap ~20 f. Freeze attacker and target together; consider no hitstop on the player's *own* blocked/parried hits beyond a few frames (Space Marine 2 complaint).
- **Monster Hunter rule** (hitstop only on charge Level 2+) maps directly onto the prototype's chargeable heavy: make hitstop and shake scale per charge level so the charge "pays out" audibly and visibly.
- **Buffer:** use a ~6–9 f buffer window (fighting-game range) rather than ER's whole-animation queue; **clear the buffer on getting hit/staggered**; a dodge press should **overwrite** (not queue behind) a buffered attack. This preserves ER's committed feel while avoiding its most-cited annoyance.
- **Animation canceling:** Souls-style weight comes from *not* allowing attack→dodge cancels during startup/active frames; allow cancel only in late recovery. (Inference; ResetEra posters confirm ER has no general cancel system.)
- **Screen shake:** trauma model; light hits ~0.1–0.2 trauma (nearly invisible after squaring), charged heavy 0.4–0.6, boss slam / poise break 0.7–1.0; directional shake along the hit vector suits side-view.
- **Sound:** charged heavy needs a rising charge loop + release whoosh + layered impact with low end; for a stick-figure prototype, audio and hitstop will carry most of the perceived weight because visuals are minimal.

- No verified Elden Ring buffer window length in frames.
- Could not verify the SFV 8/12/15 figures; treat as low confidence.
- Unverified (background knowledge, not confirmed this session): Hollow Knight briefly freezes the game with a flash when the Knight takes damage, emphasizing being hit rather than hitting; Capcom said it would adjust Great Sword hitstop for Wilds' full release after beta feedback.

## Q6. What makes a 2D souls-like "feel like Dark Souls / Elden Ring" — or fail to

### Takeaway
Players/critics credit "Souls feel" to **committed, stamina-limited actions; wind-ups and timing over mashing; hard-hitting enemies; readable telegraphs answered by dodge/parry; and hit feedback (hitstop/impact)**. Failures cited: weightless/no hitstop, detached attacks, stiff movement, over-punishing stamina, roll-spam degeneracy, and boss HP sponges.

### Cited Findings
- Positive "Souls feel" markers: Death's Gambit "feels and plays like Dark Souls in 2d" (Low, forum) — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/356650/discussions/0/1743343017633953680/?l=spanish); wind-ups emphasize "timing over button-mashing" (Med) — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/deaths-gambit-review); Elderand players "settle into a more strategic, almost Souls-like rhythm" because enemies hit hard (Med) — [Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/491838/elderand-review-a-satisfying-monster-slaying-action-platformer/); Ender Lilies is "focused on defense, dodging and finding just a right window to strike" and "punishes impatience" (Med) — [COGconnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/ender-lilies-quietus-of-the-knights-review/)
- Grime judged an "overall successful transposition of the 2D Soulslike subgenre" (Metacritic summary). (Med) — [Metacritic](https://metacritic.com/game/grime/)
- Failure markers: Ender Lilies "weightless" due to lack of hitstop and "combat can feel detached" (Low/Med) — [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1369630/discussions/2/3199241400248821683/); Elderand movement "incredibly stiff" (Med) — [WellPlayed](https://www.well-played.com.au/elderand-review/); Mandragora "responsive if not incredibly impactful" and stamina so punishing you get only a few swings (Med) — [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/17387-mandragora-whispers-of-the-witch-tree-review); Death's Gambit stamina "too restrictive" (Med) — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/deaths-gambit-review); Silksong boss HP causing attrition fights (Med) — [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/review/hollow-knight-silksong/); roll degenerates into i-frame spam in 2D (Med) — [Problem Machine](https://www.patreon.com/ProblemMachine/posts/rolled-flat-70625048); Monster Hunter Wilds beta charge attacks lacking hitstop felt less impactful (Med) — [Deltia's Gaming](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=113987)
- Kotaku's 2D-specific friction: misjudging roll-through distance and bumping into enemies. (Med) — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/1765579567)

### Inferences
- For the stick-figure prototype, the highest-leverage ER-feel items not yet present are, in rough order: (1) **hitstop + shake + layered audio scaled to the heavy's charge level** (fixes the most-cited "weightless" failure); (2) **jump** (the 2D substitute for circling and the answer to sweeps; also enables an ER-style jump heavy); (3) **backstep** as a short non-crossing spacing tool so walking/rolling don't collapse into roll spam; (4) **guard/parry → guard counter → stance-break critical** to complete the poise-break loop already in the prototype; (5) **tight input buffer** that clears on hit and lets dodge overwrite queued attacks.
- Keep stamina meaningful but avoid Mandragora/Death's Gambit-level stinginess: critics accept stamina gating when a full light combo + one roll is affordable, and complain when only "a few swings" are possible (inference from the critiques above).
- Boss design: telegraph clarity and "hits hard" matter more than HP; avoid HP-sponge phase 2 (Silksong critique).

### Gaps
- No systematic player survey or postmortem found that quantifies what makes 2D souls-likes "feel like Elden Ring" specifically; evidence is review quotes and forum anecdotes.
- No developer postmortem (Ska, The Game Kitchen, Red Candle, Team Cherry) with tuning numbers was retrievable in this session.

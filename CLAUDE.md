# Working rules for the Stick Souls project (`stick-souls/`)

1. Don't agree with the user blindly. Be critical of every suggestion or idea they bring, and give your own point of view with the reasons.
2. Don't assume. Always ask when there are gaps.
3. Base suggestions and answers on the souls games (this project only).
4. Every suggestion, idea or change applied to the game must take into account that the game is 2D, not 3D.
5. When a 2D-specific mechanic would work better than the one it replaces from the 3D souls games, propose it and give your point of view with the reasons.
